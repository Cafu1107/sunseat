# Changelog

All notable changes to Sunseat. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added
- **Park mode:** a 24-direction rose shows which way the nose should face so the steering wheel stays coolest, plus the cabin temperature when you return (best vs worst direction).
- **Alternative routes:** up to 3 routes, the shadiest one for your seat is marked; click a card or the dashed line on the map to switch.
- **Precise location picking:** fixed-pin "pick on map" mode with live address, "Start here / Go here" on map tap, GPS, pasted coordinates, recent places.
- **Paint colour** and a **cabin temperature** chart (what the cabin would reach with the AC off).
- **My car builder:** body, roof (metal, panoramic, all glass, soft top), privacy glass, AC, 2/5/7 seats. New Hyundai i20 and 7-seat Dacia Jogger.
- **Tan map** with sunscreen advice, **four-season** comparison table.
- **Live sky** behind the top-down car: blue at noon, orange at sunset, stars at night.
- **Saved routes** and **15 hidden badges**.
- **PWA:** installable, app shell works offline, routes and weather you viewed before are cached.

### Fixed
- Place search no longer pulls results towards Ankara ("Sapanca" used to find a village near Polatlı); towns rank above same-named villages, native names are used, house numbers and neighbourhoods are shown.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added
- First release: route + departure time + car model in, minute-by-minute sun exposure per seat out.
- Top-down car with the sun orbiting it, per-seat timelines, map coloured by your seat's sun.
- Best departure time (±3 h), glare warnings, driver arm index, family mode, Şahin sweat index, share card, TR/EN, light/dark.
